# Losses are compositional

A constructive proved model of structural loss fixing,
in the family of software results proved in Lean.

A system's contract is derived from its recorded
behavior; recording is additive; contracts compose with
systems; the composed contract projects onto quantifiable
solvable axes of capability, design, and performance;
gaps compose compoundingly from component gaps and
recorded interaction gaps; loss freeness is compositional;
and every loss in a whole localizes to a gap at a named
contract or composition site. A loss is fixed at its
site, not at the instance.

## Contents

- The spec file: settled intent, the proven list, the
  controls, the named premises, the open proposals, and
  the executable eight step method with its measures.
- The definitions file: every term of the model at
  implementer resolution, tied to its Lean counterpart.
- The proof folder: the Lean core (thirteen theorems), the
  standing controls (three proved negations over concrete
  counterexamples), the family check gate, and its log.
  Lean 4.19.0; zero sorries; warnings as errors; printed
  axioms within the standard set.

## Honest bounds

The model proves structure. It flips no beat cell: a cell
flips only on a same-contract re-measure at a current
pin. Record fidelity for a concrete campaign is that
campaign's evidence, named as a premise in the spec.
